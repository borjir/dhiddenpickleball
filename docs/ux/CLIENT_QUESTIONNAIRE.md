# Client Decisions Questionnaire

**Project:** D-Hidden Pickleball Court & Bistro booking website
**Phase:** Phase 2 follow-up — business decisions; partially returned
**Version:** 1.3
**Date:** 4 November 2026
**Prepared by:** Project team

This is the short version of `docs/ux/CLIENT_DECISIONS.md`. It asks only for
decisions the project team cannot make for the club. It is not a contract and it
does not change any policy by itself.

**Status note (4 November 2026):** an exported response was returned and
reconciled. Two rows were present; the row dated 30 October 2026 ("Management")
was adopted as authoritative.

On 4 November 2026 the project owner also answered the three flow blockers and
the location questions: a 15-minute hold, a payment deadline equal to that hold,
full cancellation outcomes, the address and map pin, the publishable phone and
email, and the name "D-Hidden Pickleball". Those are recorded under *Owner
decisions* in `docs/ux/CLIENT_DECISIONS.md` version 2.1 and are still open to
written confirmation from the club.

What remains unanswered: weekend rate and rounding, booking window and buffer,
maximum players, holiday closures, facilities, open play, staff accounts, the
club's real GCash QR and receiving account name, social links, and privacy and
retention wording.

## How to answer

You do not need to edit the repository or read the full UX plan.

1. Reply in chat or email using the question numbers, or
2. Print/annotate this file, or
3. Ask us to walk through it with you.

For each question:

- Answer in plain language.
- If you do not know yet, write **DEFER**. We will keep it as an open item.
- If you have no preference, write **DELEGATE**. We will propose a default for
  your approval; it will not become a club rule until you confirm it.
- Leave the answer blank only if you want us to follow up.

Please **do not send passwords, OTPs, card numbers, or bank credentials**.
Share QR images and receiving-account details through a secure folder or
approved file-sharing method, not a public repository.

**If you only have time for five answers, please answer Q2, Q3, Q4, Q6, and Q7.**
They affect the core booking and payment screens the most.
